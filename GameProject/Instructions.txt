HOW TO PLAY
-----------
- Enter your username and age (12-99) to start.
- Choose how to travel to school: walking, bicycle, bus or car.
- During the trip, answer the questions by typing one of the options
  shown in the brackets, for example yes/no or hill/road.
- After each event a small menu appears:
    backpack       - show what you are carrying
    location       - show the route map and where you are
    continue/enter - go on with the trip (typing "continue" or just
                     pressing Enter both work)
    quit           - leave the game


SAVING
------
Your progress is saved automatically at the start of every event.
To continue later, start the game, enter the same username and answer
"yes" to "Continue the saved game?". You continue from the start of
the event you were on.
