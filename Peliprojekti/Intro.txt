==============================================
          ONE MORNING, MANY CHOICES
==============================================

It is a normal school morning. The alarm has gone off, the sun is
shining, and you are still chilling on the couch at home.

How will you get to school today? Every route has its own surprises,
and every little choice you make changes how your morning ends.

Will you arrive on time? Will you make good decisions, or a mess?
There is only one way to find out.
